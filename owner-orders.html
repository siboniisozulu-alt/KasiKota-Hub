How real order management will work

  • Receive an order submitted by a customer.
  • Accept it or reject it with a reason.
  • Tell the customer about delays.
  • Mark the order ready for collection or out for delivery.
  • Mark the order completed when fulfilled.

These real functions require secure order storage, owner access controls, and a connection to the customer tracking page.
