Frequently asked questions

How do I order a kota?

Search for a shop, open its menu, add items to your basket, choose delivery or collection and prepare your order.

Which areas does Kota Connect cover?

The website includes Newcastle CBD, Madadeni Sections 1–7, Osizweni and an option for other Newcastle areas. Actual shop coverage depends on registered businesses.

How much is delivery?

The displayed delivery fee is R15 per order. The shop must confirm delivery availability and the fee before the order is accepted.

How can I pay?

Payment choices include cash on delivery, cash on collection, EFT by arrangement and electronic payment if supported. Online payments require a secure payment provider.

How do I register my kota shop?

Complete the shop registration form with your business name, location and contact details. Registrations should be reviewed before the shop is made public.

How does shop approval work?

This prototype records a pending status. Real approval requires a private administrator account, server-side review and a shared database. A browser-only status is not proof of approval.

Can I add more shops?

There is no fixed shop count in this code. However, this browser-only prototype saves data locally. A shared database is needed for many shops and customers to use the same live listings.

Is my personal information private?

Do not enter sensitive banking information. A live version should protect customer details, restrict address access to the relevant seller and use secure connections and access controls.

Can I cancel or change an order?

Contact the shop directly to request a change or cancellation. This prototype does not send orders to shops automatically.
